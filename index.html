Plutchik’s Wheel of Emotions

Robert Plutchik developed a model that organizes emotions around eight basic emotion families:

Joy, Trust, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger, and Anticipation.

The model also represents differences in emotional intensity. For example, the Fear family ranges from Apprehension → Fear → Terror, while the Joy family ranges from Serenity → Joy → Ecstasy.

The wheel also places related and contrasting emotions in relation to one another. Emotion Explorer uses this framework as a structure for reflection and emotion labeling.

Emotion Explorer is an exploratory self-reflection tool inspired by this framework. It is not a standardized psychological assessment and does not provide a medical or psychological diagnosis.

Reference

Robert Plutchik. The Nature of Emotions. American Scientist, 89(4), 344–350, 2001.
